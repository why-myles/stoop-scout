# Stoop Scout

Free stuff hits NYC curbs all day — [@stoopingnyc](https://www.instagram.com/stoopingnyc/) spots it, and this page watches it for you.

Open the page, set your wishlist (tables, seating, lamps, kitchen stuff, your own keywords) and your corners of the city, and finds near you get flagged with distances. Your settings are saved only on your own device — everyone's page is their own.

The finds feed (`finds.json`) is refreshed about every 30 minutes from @stoopingnyc's latest posts.

**Live page:** https://why-myles.github.io/stoop-scout/
